# Prioritized Backlog

Use Priority values `P0` (critical), `P1` (high), `P2` (medium), or `P3` (low). Score Business Value and Technical Complexity from 1 (low) to 5 (high). Prefer high-value, low-complexity work while reserving P0 for urgent risk or business continuity. Status values: `Proposed`, `Ready`, `In Progress`, `Blocked`, or `Done`.

| Epic | Feature | Priority | Business Value | Technical Complexity | Status |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Foundation | Define first project increment | P1 | 5 | 1 | Ready |
